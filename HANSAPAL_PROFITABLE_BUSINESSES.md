## Detailed Business Profiles & Preferable Scheme Structuring

### [BIZ-01] Solar-Powered Milk Chilling & Paneer/Curd Processing Unit
- **Sector / Category:** Dairy & Value Addition
- **Preferable Scheme:** **Mukhyamantri Krushi Udyog Yojana (MKUY)** (`SCHEME-MKUY-18`)
- **Alternative Schemes:** PMFME (35% Subsidy), Commercial Term Loan Scheme
- **Financial Structuring:** Total Project Cost: **₹480,000** | Beneficiary Contribution (10%): **₹48,000** | Loan Financed (90%): **₹432,000**
- **Effective Interest Rate & Terms:** `Commercial bank rate with 40-50% back-ended capital subsidy` | Tenure: **7 Years (6-month moratorium)**
- **Direct Subsidy / Incentive:** **₹1,92,000 to ₹2,40,000 (40% for General, 50% for Women/SC/ST)**
- **Why It Succeeds in Hansapal:** Adjacent Balianta block produces surplus milk, but farmers sell to middlemen at low rates (₹32-34/L). A chilling and paneer unit at Hansapal can supply fresh paneer/curd directly to Bhubaneswar sweet shops & restaurants at ₹280-320/kg.
- **Target Customer Base:** Bhubaneswar caterers, sweet shops, Rasulgarh wholesale buyers
- **ROI & Payback Timeline:** 18 - 24 months
- **Community Impact:** High (Empowers 15-20 local dairy farmers with higher milk procurement prices)
