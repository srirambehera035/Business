## 2. In-Depth Scheme Profiles & Implementation Parameters

### [SCHEME-MFS-01] Micro Finance Scheme
- **Implementing Agency:** State Channelizing Agencies (SCAs) / NBFC-MFIs / Scheduled Banks
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `6.5% p.a. (under Government Concessional SCA window); Market MFI rate: 16% - 26% p.a.`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Individual: up to ₹2.00 Lakh (Concessional SCA cap: ₹1.25 Lakh - ₹1.40 Lakh); Large MFIs/NBFC-MFIs: up to ₹1,000 Crore credit line
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `10% beneficiary contribution (under state concessional channels)`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **Small loans (under ₹25,000): 12 - 18 months; Loans above ₹30,000: 24 - 36 months (up to 3 years)** | Moratorium: **3 months repayment holiday directly after disbursement**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Total annual household income must not exceed ₹3,00,000
  * Age limit: 18 - 60 years
  * Loans provided to individuals or through Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) and Self Help Groups (SHGs)
  * Zero physical collateral required
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * KYC documents (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card)
  * Photographs of borrower and co-borrower / guarantor
  * 6 months bank account statement
  * Ration card or local income certificate
  * End-use declaration of funds
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Small retail / Kirana, Backyard poultry / Dairy, Handicrafts & Tailoring, Street food & local services

### [SCHEME-TLS-02] Term Loan Scheme (Concessional SCA & MSME Window)
- **Implementing Agency:** Apex Corporations (NSFDC / NMDFC / NBCFDC) & Partner Commercial Banks
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `8.0% p.a. (Apex Concessional Window); Commercial MSME rate: 9% - 13% depending on credit rating`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** NMDFC: up to ₹30 Lakh; NSFDC: up to ₹45 Lakh; PMMY Tarun: up to ₹20 Lakh; Commercial Bank MSME term loan: up to ₹20 Crore
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `10% - 15% beneficiary margin contribution`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **3 to 7 years (up to 10 years for agro-processing; up to 25 years for large fixed infrastructure)** | Moratorium: **3 to 12 months (typically 6 months for agro-processing and manufacturing units)**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Business must display consistent profitability for at least 2 consecutive financial years (for existing units)
  * CIBIL credit score of 700 or above preferred
  * Age limit: 21 - 65 years
  * Eligible entities: Sole proprietorships, partnership firms, private limited companies, LLPs
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Audited balance sheets and P&L statements for last 2-3 years
  * ITR and GST returns for past 1-2 years
  * KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID)
  * Business proof: Udyam registration, partnership deed, MOA/AOA, trade license
  * Detailed Project Report (DPR) with machinery quotations
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Light manufacturing, Food processing & packaging, Commercial dairy chilling, Fabrication & warehousing

### [SCHEME-MSY-03] Mahila Samriddhi Yojana (MSY)
- **Implementing Agency:** National Minorities / Backward Classes / SC Corporations via State SCAs
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `4.0% per annum (Highly subsidized concessional rate)`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Individual beneficiary: up to ₹1,25,000; SHGs / Group loans: up to ₹15,00,000
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `5% - 10% (Subsidized channel)`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **Up to 5 years (60 months)** | Moratorium: **Up to 6 months**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Applicant must be a woman
  * Age limit: 18 to 55 years
  * Target category: Backward Classes (OBC), SC, ST, or Below Poverty Line (BPL)
  * Self-employment intent: Must use loan to start or expand a micro-enterprise
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Identity proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID)
  * Address proof (Electricity bill, Ration card, Residence certificate)
  * Income certificate issued by competent revenue authority
  * Caste / Category certificate
  * Bank passbook details
  * Passport size photographs
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Tailoring & boutique, Pickle / Papad / Food packaging, Beauty parlor, Bangle & ornament trading

### [SCHEME-MKSP-04] Mahila Kisan Sashaktikaran Pariyojana (MKSP)
- **Implementing Agency:** Ministry of Rural Development (DAY-NRLM) / State Rural Livelihoods Missions (OLM in Odisha)
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `Standard concessional rate: 7% p.a.; Prompt repayment subvention: 4% p.a.; Special Odisha State Subvention (Mission Shakti): 0% p.a.`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Individual woman farmer: up to ₹3,00,000; SHG Federation: up to ₹10,00,000
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `Nil or nominal SHG group corpus contribution`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **3 years for single project cycle** | Moratorium: **Internal SHG loans: up to 6 months; Large individual enterprise loans: up to 18 months; Crop failure relief: 1-2 years**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Must be a rural woman farmer
  * Must be an active member of a registered Self-Help Group (SHG)
  * Both land-owning farmers and landless agricultural wage laborers are eligible
  * Age: 18 years or older
  * Statutory reservation: ST (15%), SC (25%)
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Identity and address proof
  * SHG membership resolution and passbook copy
  * Bank account details
  * Land / work cultivation records
  * Caste certificate (for SC/ST claims)
  * Ration card / BPL card
  * Passport size photograph
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Organic vegetable farming, Mushroom production, Poultry & goat rearing, Bio-inputs & composting

### [SCHEME-AMY-05] Aajeevika Microfinance Yojana (AMY)
- **Implementing Agency:** State Scheduled Castes Finance & Development Corporation (OSFDC in Odisha)
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `5.0% per annum`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Up to ₹1,25,000 (with remaining ₹15,000 as promoter contribution / margin money)
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `10% (e.g., ₹14,000 on a ₹1,40,000 unit)`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **3 years (36 monthly installments)** | Moratorium: **3 months**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Caste: Must belong to Scheduled Caste (SC) community
  * No ceiling on family annual income
  * Age limit: 18 to 60 years
  * Loan must be utilized for income-generating micro-business activities
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Caste certificate issued by Tehsildar / Sub-Collector
  * Identity proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID)
  * Address proof (Ration card / Electricity bill)
  * Age proof (School certificate or Birth certificate)
  * Bank account passbook copy
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Sanitary hardware trading, Footwear repair / Leather craft, Two-wheeler mechanic garage, Kirana store

### [SCHEME-MAY-06] Mahila Adhikarita Yojana (MAY)
- **Implementing Agency:** National Safai Karamcharis Finance & Development Corporation (NSKFDC)
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `1.0% to 4.0% per annum (Lowest tier concessional lending)`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Up to ₹2,00,000 (100% financial assistance available)
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `Nil or up to 5%`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **Up to 5 years (60 months)** | Moratorium: **Up to 6 months**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Applicant must be a Safai Karamchari (cleaning/sanitation worker or manual scavenger)
  * Only women are eligible (including dependent daughters of Safai Karamcharis)
  * Age: 18 years old or above
  * Must be a citizen of India and utilize funds for small business or trade
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Identity and address proof
  * Proof of profession (Safai Karamchari ID card / local municipality certification)
  * Caste certificate
  * Brief project profile / simple business plan
  * Bank account details and photographs
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Flour / Spice milling, Sanitary napkin packaging, Vegetable retail cart, Readymade garment trading

### [SCHEME-MUDRA-07] Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)
- **Implementing Agency:** Commercial Banks / RRBs / Small Finance Banks / MFIs
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `8.0% to 12.5% p.a. (Determined by lending bank based on risk grade)`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Categorized into 4 tiers: Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (₹50,001 to ₹5,00,000), Tarun (₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000), Tarun Plus (₹10,00,001 to ₹20,00,000 for previous Tarun repayers)
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `Shishu: Nil; Kishore & Tarun: 10% - 15%`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **Loans up to ₹5 Lakh: up to 5 years; Loans up to ₹20 Lakh: up to 7 years** | Moratorium: **Shishu: Up to 3 months; Kishore/Tarun: 6 to 12 months**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Target borrowers: Non-Corporate Small Business (NCSB) segment in rural and urban areas
  * Age limit: 18 to 65 years
  * Must have a satisfactory repayment record and no existing default
  * Zero physical collateral and zero third-party guarantee required
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Identity and address proof
  * Business registration proof (Udyam, GST, Trade license)
  * Bank account statements for past 6-12 months
  * Financial statements / ITR (for Kishore and Tarun tiers)
  * Machinery purchase quotations / Project report
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Retail trading, Artisans & food stalls, Transport vehicle (Auto/Pick-up van), Repair workshops

### [SCHEME-SUI-08] Stand-Up India Scheme
- **Implementing Agency:** All Scheduled Commercial Banks (Mandated 1 SC/ST & 1 Woman per branch)
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `Lowest applicable rate for that category: Base Rate (MCLR) + 3% + tenure premium`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Between ₹10.00 Lakh and ₹1.00 Crore
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `Up to 15% (can be converged with eligible state capital subsidies to bring promoter margin to 10%)`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **Up to 7 years** | Moratorium: **Up to 18 months**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Target group: Women entrepreneurs (all categories) and SC/ST entrepreneurs
  * Age: 18 years or above
  * Must be a greenfield enterprise (first time venture in manufacturing, services, agri-allied, or trading)
  * At least 51% shareholding and controlling stake must be held by SC/ST or woman promoter
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Identity and address proof
  * Caste certificate (for SC/ST category)
  * Greenfield project report (Detailed Project Report)
  * Balance sheets and projected cash flows
  * Proof of business premises (rent lease / land ownership)
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Agro-cold chain, Industrial fabrication, Textile garmenting units, Diagnostic laboratories

### [SCHEME-SVANIDHI-09] PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi)
- **Implementing Agency:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) via Scheduled Banks / NBFCs
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `Base bank interest rate (9.9% - 15% p.a.) with a direct 7.0% p.a. interest subsidy credited to account + ₹1,200/yr digital cashback`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** 1st Tranche: up to ₹15,000; 2nd Tranche: up to ₹25,000; 3rd Tranche: up to ₹50,000
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `0% (Nil margin money)`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **1st Tranche: 12 months; 2nd Tranche: 18 months; 3rd Tranche: 36 months** | Moratorium: **Nil (Immediate monthly repayment to build credit discipline)**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Targets street vendors operating in urban as well as surrounding peri-urban and rural fringe areas
  * Must be actively engaged in vending
  * Must possess a Certificate of Vending (CoV) / Identity Card issued by Town Vending Committee (TVC), or a Letter of Recommendation (LoR) approved by ULB/BDO
  * Age limit: 18 to 60 years
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Aadhaar Card / Voter ID
  * Certificate of Vending (CoV) / Vending ID / BDO Letter of Recommendation
  * Bank account passbook copy
  * Registered mobile number linked with Aadhaar
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Street food carts, Vegetable / Fruit vending, Flower & garland stall, Newspaper / Cobbler stalls

### [SCHEME-VISHWAKARMA-10] PM Vishwakarma Scheme
- **Implementing Agency:** Ministry of MSME & Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `Concessional fixed rate of 5.0% per annum (Government pays up to 8% interest subvention to lending bank)`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** 1st Tranche: up to ₹1,00,000; 2nd Tranche: up to ₹2,00,000; Total: up to ₹3,00,000
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `0% beneficiary contribution`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **1st Tranche: up to 18 months; 2nd Tranche: up to 30 months** | Moratorium: **As per bank repayment schedule (typically 1 to 3 months)**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Must be an artisan or craftsperson working with hands and tools in one of the 18 recognized traditional trades (Carpenter, Blacksmith, Potter, Mason, Barber, Tailor, Cobbler, etc.)
  * Minimum age: 18 years
  * Self-employed in the unorganized / informal sector
  * Only one member per family is eligible
  * Must not have availed loans under PMEGP, PM SVANidhi, or MUDRA in the past 5 years
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Aadhaar Card linked with mobile number
  * Bank account details (IFSC & Account number)
  * PM Vishwakarma portal registration & biometrics
  * Trade verification by Gram Panchayat Pradhan or Urban Local Body Executive Officer
  * Ration card
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Carpentry, Masonry (Rajmistri), Pottery, Blacksmithing, Barber / Hair salon, Tailoring, Cobbler / Leathercraft

### [SCHEME-KCC-11] Kisan Credit Card (KCC) - Crop, Dairy, Poultry & Fisheries
- **Implementing Agency:** NABARD / Commercial Banks / Cooperative Banks / RRBs
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `Standard 7% p.a. concessional rate; 3% Prompt Repayment Incentive (Effective cost to farmer: 4.0% p.a.)`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Collateral-free credit limit: up to ₹2,00,000 (elevated from ₹1.60L); Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS) limit: up to ₹5,00,000 (dairy/poultry/crop)
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `Nil for loans up to ₹2.00 Lakh`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **Card valid for 5 years with annual renewal; repayment linked to seasonal marketing cycle** | Moratorium: **Linked to crop harvesting / livestock lactation cycle (no rigid monthly moratorium)**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Individual farmers, tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers
  * Self Help Groups (SHGs) or Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) of farmers
  * Farmers involved in agriculture, animal husbandry, dairy cattle rearing, poultry, or fisheries
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Aadhaar card / KYC documents
  * Land ownership record (RoR / Patta) or lease agreement / tenancy declaration
  * Bank account details and photograph
  * Proof of livestock ownership (ear-tag records) for dairy/poultry KCC
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Dairy farming (Cattle feed & maintenance), Broiler poultry farming, Paddy & vegetable cultivation, Fisheries culture

### [SCHEME-PMFME-12] PM Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PMFME)
- **Implementing Agency:** Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) & State Nodal Agencies
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `Lending bank's prevailing commercial MSME interest rate (effectively cushioned by the 35% upfront capital subsidy)`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Individual units: Credit-linked capital subsidy of 35% of eligible project cost (Maximum ₹10.00 Lakh); Common infrastructure: 35% subsidy up to ₹3.00 Crore
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `10% beneficiary contribution`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **Generally up to 6 years (term loan)** | Moratorium: **Up to 6 months depending on commercial bank sanction**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Individual micro food processing enterprises (existing units upgrading or new units)
  * Proprietorship / partnership firms, FPOs, Farmer Producer Companies, Cooperatives, and SHGs
  * Focus on 'One District One Product' (ODOP) - e.g., Fish/Prawn in Khordha, Turmeric in Kandhamal
  * Applicant must possess ownership / lease of premises
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Aadhaar & KYC documents
  * Detailed Project Report (DPR) prepared via District Resource Person (DRP)
  * Business registration (Udyam / FSSAI license / GST if applicable)
  * Machinery quotations and factory floor layout
  * Bank statements for last 12 months
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Bakery & confectionery, Mustard oil expeller, Spice grinding & packaging, Puffed rice (Mudhi) making, Pickle & sauce processing

### [SCHEME-PMEGP-13] Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)
- **Implementing Agency:** Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC) / DIC / KVIB
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `Normal bank MSME interest rate, offset by substantial non-refundable government margin money subsidy: Urban (15% Gen, 25% Spl); Rural (25% Gen, 35% Spl)`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Manufacturing Sector: Maximum project cost up to ₹50.00 Lakh; Service / Business Sector: Maximum project cost up to ₹20.00 Lakh
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `Beneficiary margin: 10% for General category; 5% for Special Categories (SC/ST/OBC/Women/Rural)`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **3 to 7 years** | Moratorium: **Initial moratorium of 6 months as prescribed by the financing bank**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Applicant must be 18 years or above
  * Minimum 8th pass qualification for manufacturing projects costing >₹10 Lakh and service projects >₹5 Lakh
  * Must set up a new micro-enterprise in the non-farm sector
  * Special categories: SC, ST, OBC, Women, Minorities, Ex-servicemen, PwD
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Aadhaar Card and passport photographs
  * Educational qualification certificate (8th pass certificate/marksheet)
  * Caste / Category certificate
  * Rural Area Certificate issued by Gram Panchayat / BDO
  * Detailed Project Report (DPR) with machine quotes
  * EDP (Entrepreneurship Development Training) certificate
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Fly-ash bricks & paver blocks, Woodwork & furniture, Corrugated packaging, Food products, Automobile repair garage

### [SCHEME-NBCFDC-14] NBCFDC Concessional Credit Schemes
- **Implementing Agency:** National Backward Classes Finance and Development Corporation (via State SCAs)
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `Up to ₹1.25 Lakh: 7.0% p.a.; From ₹1.25 Lakh to ₹25 Lakh: 8.0% p.a.; 1.0% rebate for timely repayments`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Individual loans: up to ₹25.00 Lakh (Up to 85% of project cost financed by Corporation)
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `15% promoter margin (can be reduced to 10% under special state schemes)`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **Up to ₹1.25 Lakh: 4 years; ₹1.25 Lakh to ₹25 Lakh: up to 7 years** | Moratorium: **1 quarter (3 months)**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Must belong to an eligible Other Backward Class (OBC) notified by Central or State Government
  * Annual family income must not exceed ₹3,00,000
  * Must undertake a viable income-generating micro-enterprise or service activity
  * Persons with Disabilities (PwDs) receive an additional 0.25% interest concession
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Aadhaar card / Identity proof
  * OBC caste certificate
  * Income certificate (annual family income < ₹3 Lakh)
  * Detailed project report / business quotation
  * Bank passbook details
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Agri-transport vehicles, Dairy & livestock, Handicrafts & tailoring, Small hardware stores

### [SCHEME-NHFDC-15] NHFDC Concessional Loan Scheme for Divyangjan
- **Implementing Agency:** National Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation via State SCAs & RRBs
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `Loans up to ₹50,000: 5.0% p.a.; ₹50,000 to ₹5 Lakh: 6.0% p.a.; ₹5 Lakh to ₹15 Lakh: 7.0% p.a.; ₹15 Lakh to ₹50 Lakh: 8.0% p.a. (Special 1% rebate for women with disabilities)`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Up to ₹50.00 Lakh per beneficiary / unit
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `Loans up to ₹5 Lakh: Nil; Loans above ₹5 Lakh: 5% - 10%`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **Up to 10 years depending on the activity** | Moratorium: **As decided by implementing agency (typically 6 to 12 months for commercial projects)**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Person with benchmark disability (minimum 40% disability certified by medical authority)
  * Indian citizen aged 18 years or above
  * Eligible for setting up self-employment ventures or commercial activities
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Unique Disability ID (UDID) Card / Medical Disability Certificate (>=40%)
  * Aadhaar card / KYC documents
  * Income & residence certificate
  * Project profile / machinery quotation
  * Bank details
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Digital service kiosk (CSC), Stationery & xerox shop, Retail grocery, Small fabrication / repair

### [SCHEME-NMDFC-16] NMDFC Concessional Term Loan (Credit Line 1 & Credit Line 2)
- **Implementing Agency:** National Minorities Development & Finance Corporation via State SCAs
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `Credit Line 1: 6.0% p.a. for both male & female; Credit Line 2: 8.0% p.a. for male, 6.0% p.a. for female beneficiaries`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Credit Line 1: up to ₹20.00 Lakh; Credit Line 2: up to ₹30.00 Lakh
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `Credit Line 1: 5% - 10%; Credit Line 2: 10% - 15%`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **Up to 5 years (60 monthly installments)** | Moratorium: **6 months**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Must belong to a notified National Minority community (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi)
  * Credit Line 1: Annual family income up to ₹3,00,000 (Targeted concessional segment)
  * Credit Line 2: Annual family income up to ₹8,00,000 (Extended segment for semi-urban entrepreneurs)
  * Must undertake an economically viable income-generating activity
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Minority community self-declaration / certificate
  * Income certificate issued by competent authority
  * Aadhaar Card and address proof
  * Detailed project report / asset quotation
  * Bank account passbook copy
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Bakery & food production, Auto-rickshaw / Light transport, Apparel & garment manufacturing, Hardware & electrical store

### [SCHEME-MSHAKTI-17] Mission Shakti 0% Interest Loan Scheme (Odisha Pilot Window)
- **Implementing Agency:** Mission Shakti Department, Government of Odisha via Scheduled Commercial Banks
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `0.0% effective interest rate (State Govt reimburses the full bank interest up to ₹10 Lakh upon regular repayment)`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Up to ₹10.00 Lakh loan with 100% interest subvention (Zero Interest)
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `0% margin contribution`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **3 to 5 years** | Moratorium: **Up to 3 months**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Must be a registered Women Self Help Group (WSHG) in Odisha
  * Group must have completed at least 6 months of active savings and internal lending
  * Prompt repayment track record
  * Directly applicable to peri-urban & rural women in Khordha/Bhubaneswar blocks
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * WSHG registration certificate & resolution book
  * Bank account passbook of the SHG
  * Aadhaar cards of President and Secretary
  * Micro-credit business plan / enterprise proposal
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Mushroom cultivation, Sal leaf plate making, Spices processing, Rural tiffin kitchen, Mid-day meal catering

### [SCHEME-MKUY-18] Mukhyamantri Krushi Udyog Yojana (MKUY)
- **Implementing Agency:** APICOL & Department of Agriculture & Farmers' Empowerment, Government of Odisha
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `Bank commercial agriculture loan rate, compensated by direct 40%-50% back-ended capital subsidy`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Capital Investment Subsidy (CIS) of 40% of fixed capital (up to ₹50.00 Lakh); 50% subsidy for SC/ST/Women/Agri-graduates
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `10% to 20% promoter contribution`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **5 to 7 years** | Moratorium: **6 to 12 months**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Any individual entrepreneur, farmer, or agri-graduate setting up a commercial agro-enterprise in Odisha
  * Must register on the GO-SUGOM / MKUY portal before commencing project
  * Greenfield or expansion of commercial dairy, goatry, food processing, cold room, nursery
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Detailed Project Report (DPR) approved by APICOL
  * Land possession documents (RoR or minimum 10-year registered lease)
  * Aadhaar, PAN, Bank sanction letter
  * Machinery quotations and building estimate
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Commercial dairy farm (>=10 cows), Milk chilling & paneer processing, Biofloc fisheries, Commercial horticulture nursery

### [SCHEME-SURYAGHAR-19] PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana (Rooftop Solar for Micro-Units)
- **Implementing Agency:** Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) via DISCOMs & National Solar Portal
- **Interest Rate Structure:** `~7.0% p.a. concessional green credit through SBI, PNB, Canara Bank`
- **Loan / Assistance Ceiling:** Direct Central Government Capital Subsidy: ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above; Collateral-free bank loan at repo + 0.5% (~7% p.a.)
- **Beneficiary Margin Money Requirement:** `10% promoter contribution`
- **Tenure & Moratorium:** Max Tenure: **Up to 10 years** | Moratorium: **3 months**
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Micro-enterprises, dairy farmers, and home-based units operating in premises with a registered electricity consumer connection
  * Sufficient shadow-free rooftop or shed space
- **Mandatory Documents Required:**
  * Electricity bill of the premises
  * Aadhaar Card and bank account details
  * Site photograph and roof feasibility report via empaneled solar vendor
- **Ideal Business Sectors:** Solar-powered cold storage, Solar milk chilling, Atta chaki / Flour mill, Sewing machine units
