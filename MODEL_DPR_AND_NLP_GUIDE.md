# Advanced Engineering Datasets: Model DPRs, NLP Lexicon & Saturation Engine
**Project:** AI-Driven Hyper-Local Business Advisory and Financial Structuring Assistant  
**Coverage:** Modules 1 & 2 Complete Data Foundation  

---

## 1. Model DPRs & Cashflow Modeling (`model_dpr_financial_profiles.json`)
Provides auditable, institutional-grade Detailed Project Reports aligned with NABARD and KVIC templates:
- **Capex Breakdown:** Machinery models, sheds, solar power kits with exact price tags.
- **Opex / Working Capital:** 15-day to 30-day raw material reserves, utility bills, packaging, and wages.
- **Monthly Revenue Projections:** Unit product sales, gross revenues, operating cashflows, and net profit.
- **Debt Structuring:** 90% Loan EMI post-moratorium, Break-even month, and Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR).

## 2. Multilingual Financial Lexicon (`multilingual_financial_lexicon.json`)
Empowers the conversational NLP chatbot to bridge the literacy divide:
- **Languages:** English, Hindi (हिन्दी), Odia (ଓଡ଼ିଆ), Bengali (বাংলা), Telugu (తెలుగు), Tamil (தமிழ்).
- **Core Concepts Simplified:** Plain rural analogies for *Margin Money*, *Moratorium*, *Concessional Interest*, *Capital Subsidy*, and *Working Capital*.
- **Intent Samples:** Pre-labeled voice/text user queries mapped to automated scheme parameters.

## 3. Saturation Benchmarks Matrix (`industry_saturation_benchmarks.json`)
Powers **Pillar 5 (Competitor Mapping & Saturation)**:
- Deterministic formula comparing local population against mapped Overpass POIs.
- Flags "High Risk Over-Saturation" or "High-Opportunity White Space" with mathematical rigor rather than vague LLM guessing.
